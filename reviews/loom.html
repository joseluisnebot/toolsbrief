What is Loom?

Loom is a video communication tool that allows users to create and share annotated videos. It enables users to add commentary and annotations to videos, making it a valuable tool for presentations, tutorials, and remote work. Loom is developed by Blind, a startup based in San Francisco. It works by allowing users to record their screen and audio, and then add annotations and comments to the video. Loom solves the problem of making it difficult to effectively communicate and share ideas in a remote work environment. It allows users to create videos that are more engaging and informative, and that can be easily shared and understood by others. Loom is available as a monthly subscription service, starting at $12.50 per month.

Who Is Loom For?

✅

Remote and async teams

Loom replaces a significant portion of meetings. Instead of scheduling a 30-minute call to explain something, record a 3-minute Loom and let people watch at their own pace.

✅

Sales teams

Personalised video outreach gets dramatically higher response rates than text emails. Loom's viewer analytics tell you exactly who watched and for how long.

❌

Professional video production

Loom is for quick async communication, not polished output. For training videos, marketing content, or anything requiring production quality, use dedicated video tools.

⚡ The Verdict

Loom: The Fastest Way to Replace a Meeting

Loom has become the default async video communication tool for good reason — it removes every friction point from recording and sharing a video. The one-click recording, instant sharing link, and automatic AI transcripts make it genuinely faster than writing a detailed email for complex topics. The free tier's 25-video limit and 5-minute cap are restrictive for regular use, which is clearly intentional. The Business plan at $12.50/month per user is reasonable for teams but adds up quickly. For solopreneurs and freelancers, the free tier covers occasional use, and the Business plan is worth it if you record more than twice a week.
