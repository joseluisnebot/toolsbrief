What is Grammarly?

Grammarly is an AI-powered writing assistant that helps users improve their grammar, spelling, and punctuation. Developed by the London-based startup Grammarly Inc., it offers a suite of tools designed to enhance written communication across various platforms, including email, social media, and documents.

The platform works by analyzing text input in real-time, suggesting corrections and providing explanations for mistakes. It uses advanced machine learning algorithms to understand context and suggest the most appropriate corrections, making it a valuable tool for individuals looking to improve their writing skills and professionals seeking to maintain a professional tone in their communications.

Grammarly solves the problem of common writing errors, helping users to communicate more effectively and professionally. It caters to a wide range of users, from students and casual writers to professionals and business leaders, making it an accessible tool for anyone who wants to enhance their writing abilities. With a monthly subscription starting at $12, Grammarly offers a cost-effective solution for those looking to improve their writing skills, making it a worthwhile investment for anyone serious about enhancing their communication skills.

Who Is Grammarly For?

✅

Non-native English speakers

Grammarly is indispensable for professionals writing in English as a second language. The grammar accuracy and natural phrasing suggestions are significantly better than built-in spell checkers.

✅

Business professionals writing emails

Tone detection and clarity suggestions make a real difference in professional communication. The free tier handles this well.

❌

Creative writers

Grammarly's suggestions can homogenise your writing voice. If you're writing fiction or personal essays, the constant 'corrections' may do more harm than good.

⚡ The Verdict

Grammarly: The Writing Assistant That Actually Works

Grammarly has earned its dominant market position by doing one thing exceptionally well: making your writing clearer and more professional with minimal effort. The free tier is genuinely useful — it handles basic grammar and catches embarrassing errors. Premium is where it gets interesting: full sentence rewrites, tone analysis, and plagiarism checking justify the cost for professionals who write daily. The annual plan at $12/month is reasonable. Monthly billing at $30/month is hard to justify. Our recommendation: use the free tier, and if you find yourself accepting suggestions daily, upgrade to annual Premium.
