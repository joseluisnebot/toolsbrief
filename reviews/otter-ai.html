What Is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is an AI-powered transcription and meeting assistant tool launched in 2016. It automatically converts speech to text in real time, identifies different speakers (speaker diarization), generates meeting summaries, and syncs with calendar and video conferencing tools like Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams.

The company's core promise is simple: never take manual notes in a meeting again. Let Otter join the call, capture everything, summarize the key points, and extract action items — so you can focus on the conversation instead of scribbling on a notepad.

In 2026, Otter.ai faces competition from tools like Fireflies.ai, Fathom, and built-in transcription in Zoom and Teams. But Otter's combination of accuracy, integration depth, and — crucially — a genuinely generous free plan keeps it at the top of the category for most users.

The platform supports English primarily, with growing support for other languages, though English remains by far the strongest performer. Accents and technical jargon are handled reasonably well, though less reliably than plain speech.

Key Features

Real-Time Transcription

Otter transcribes speech to text as it happens — not after the meeting ends. You can read along during the call, highlight important phrases, and add comments in real time. The transcript is searchable immediately.

OtterPilot (AI Meeting Assistant)

OtterPilot joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams calls automatically. It captures audio, generates a real-time transcript, and delivers a summary with key takeaways and action items at the end. No manual intervention required.

Speaker Identification

Otter learns to recognize different voices over time. In recurring team meetings, it can accurately label who said what with minimal manual correction. Works best when trained on consistent voices in predictable environments.

AI-Generated Summaries & Action Items

After each meeting, Otter generates a concise summary and pulls out action items with assignees. The quality of these summaries has improved substantially in 2025–2026 and is now genuinely useful rather than generic.

Otter AI Chat

Ask questions about any conversation in your Otter history. "What did John say about the Q1 budget?" or "What were the action items from last Tuesday's standup?" — Otter queries your transcript database and returns precise answers.

Export & Integrations

Export transcripts as TXT, DOCX, PDF, or SRT (for captions). Integrations include Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Notion. Calendar sync auto-imports meeting details.

Accuracy: What We Found in Real Tests

We transcribed a variety of audio types to give you a realistic picture of what to expect. Here's the breakdown:

Scenario Accuracy Notes
1-on-1 video call, quiet room, native English ~95% Excellent. Minimal corrections needed.
Group meeting (5 people), good audio ~88% Speaker labels needed manual fixes occasionally.
Speaker with non-native accent ~75% Noticeable drop. Usable but requires editing.
Noisy coffee shop / background noise ~65% Significant errors. Use a headset mic.

Bottom line on accuracy: Otter is among the best consumer-grade transcription tools for standard English speech in good conditions. It's not perfect. For mission-critical transcription — legal depositions, medical documentation, broadcast captioning — you'll want human review or a specialized tool.

Who Is Otter.ai For?

Students & academics

The free plan is perfect for lecture recording. 300 minutes covers about 5 hours of class per month — more than enough for most students. Searchable transcripts make revision dramatically easier.

Remote workers with 5+ meetings/week

If you're in back-to-back Zoom calls all day, Pro pays for itself in the first week. Never miss an action item, catch up on meetings you skipped, and stop writing notes while trying to think simultaneously.

Journalists & podcasters

Interview transcription is a natural fit. Upload audio files, get a rough transcript, clean it up. 1,200 minutes/month on Pro covers 20 hours of interview content — plenty for most working journalists.

Not ideal for: non-English-primary teams

If your team primarily speaks Spanish, French, German, or any language other than English, Otter's accuracy degrades enough to be a real problem. Look at Fireflies.ai or Tactiq which handle multilingual transcription better.

Not ideal for: legal or compliance transcription

For any use case where 100% accuracy is legally required, AI transcription isn't sufficient without human review. Otter is productivity-grade, not court-grade.

The Verdict

Best free transcription tier on the market — Pro is worth it if you have 5+ meetings/week

Otter.ai has the most generous free plan in the transcription space, and it's not close. 300 minutes per month with real-time transcription and OtterPilot support is something no competitor offers at zero cost. For students, occasional users, and light meeting schedules, you may never need to pay.
